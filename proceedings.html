Workshop Proceedings

Selected accepted papers presented at the workshop will be published in a special issue of the International Journal of Humanoid Robotics after necessary revisions.

World Scientific, ISSN (print): 0219-8436 | eISSN (online): 1793-6942

The International Journal of Humanoid Robotics (IJHR), published by World Scientific Publishing Co. Pte Ltd, is a peer-reviewed journal established in 2004 that focuses on both the physical (motion, structure, mechanics) and mental (perception, cognition, control) aspects of humanoid and biologically-inspired robots.
